Privacy Policy

One Upload · last updated 26 November 2026

What this app is

One Upload is a private tool used by its owner (Exploring Kerala) to post their own videos to their own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page, to schedule those posts, and to see basic statistics. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

  • Videos, captions and post settings that the owner uploads in the app.
  • Access tokens from Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook / Instagram), so the app can upload, delete and read statistics for the owner's own posts.
  • Basic channel and account details (channel name, Page name, Instagram username).

How it is used and stored

  • Data is only used to publish, schedule, delete and measure the owner's own posts.
  • Tokens and upload history are stored encrypted in the app's private storage (Vercel Blob).
  • Uploaded videos are deleted from the app's storage after they are posted.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. YouTube data is handled under the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time with "Disconnect" in the app, at Google account permissions, or in Facebook settings under Business Integrations. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions: contact the Exploring Kerala channel owner.
