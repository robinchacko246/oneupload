The service

One Upload lets you connect your own YouTube channels, Instagram professional accounts and Facebook Pages, and publish, schedule, delete and measure posts on them from one place.

Your accounts and content

  • You may only connect accounts you own or are authorised to manage.
  • You are responsible for the videos, photos and captions you publish, and they must follow the rules of YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.
  • The app only acts when you tell it to: posting now, at a time you scheduled, or deleting a post you choose.

Availability

The service is provided as is. Posts can fail if a platform rejects them or its API is unavailable; the app shows the reason and lets you retry.

Ending use

You can disconnect channels at any time on the Channels page, and ask for your account and data to be deleted as described on the data deletion page.
