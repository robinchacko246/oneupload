How to delete your data

One Upload · last updated 26 November 2026

Remove a connected Instagram account or Facebook Page

  1. Log in to One Upload and open Channels.
  2. Press Remove next to the account. Its access tokens are deleted from our database immediately.

Remove the app from Facebook

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  2. Find One Upload and choose Remove. The app can then no longer access your Pages or Instagram accounts.

Delete everything

To delete your One Upload account with all connected channels, scheduled posts, upload history and ideas, contact the app owner from the email address you signed up with and ask for account deletion. It is completed within 30 days and we confirm by email. Videos and photos you uploaded are already removed from our storage once they are posted.

See also the privacy policy.
